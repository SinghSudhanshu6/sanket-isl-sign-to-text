# Round 2 demo (from the Build Pack)
1. Open any site (WhatsApp Web, Instagram, a chatbot...), click inside its message box, open the extension side panel (or use the Studio at /demo/). Camera on.
2. Sign TODAY, WEATHER, WHAT (fallback: pick "What is the weather today?" in Quick examples).
3. The sentence forms by itself and lands in the text box (intent card details are under the sentence).
4. Sentence appears in the box; send it; select the reply text and press **Use reply / selected text → Simplify**.
5. Show /metrics/: accuracy on a signer not in training data, time-to-prompt vs typing.
Say honestly: ~30-50 signs, regional variation (mitigated by Teach + top-3 correction), no video leaves the device.
