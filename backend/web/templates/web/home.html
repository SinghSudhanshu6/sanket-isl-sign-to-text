SANKET

Giving Deaf users a voice with AI

Sign to any AI chatbot. Sanket reads Indian Sign Language on-device, turns the signs into a structured intent card, and rewrites the chatbot's answer into short, readable lines.

Try the live demo See the vocabulary
18M+

Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in India for whom ISL, not typed text, is the first language.

Intent card, not raw text

Signs become an editable card (task, recipient, topic, when). The user confirms before anything is sent.

Answers made readable

Long replies are rewritten into short lines with key words highlighted.
